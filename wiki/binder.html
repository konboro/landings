Binder

The BINDER is your collection album. You fill it by tapping real assets on the map, and it rewards you for every new catch.

In short

  • Organised by country, then category, then city.
  • You fill it only by tapping real assets on the map.
  • New categories give packs, new city cards give TC.
  • A completed country gives an Elite pack.

How it is organised

Open the BINDER tab and pick a country. Each country has category cards such as Bus, Tram, Metro, EV charger or Airport. Inside a category there is one card for each city where that category exists. Countries with landmarks also have a Landmarks card.

Not every category exists in every country. The Binder only shows the ones a country has.

How to catch

Tap a real asset on the map while it is in your range, in your agent zone or in your guild's HQ zone. The first tap in a new category catches the category, and the first tap in a new city catches that city's card. Buying an asset or pulling it from a pack doesn't fill the Binder. You have to catch it on the map.

Some categories collect more than one type: mopeds count as scooters, trains as train stations, metro trains as metro, and wind turbines go into the EV charger category.

On a category card, TAP TO CATCH shows you an asset of that type on the map.

Rewards

  • A new category in a country: a pack, from a Mini for common categories up to an Elite for airports and cable cars.
  • A new city card: about 2,000 TC.
  • A new landmark: a Standard pack.
  • A complete country: an Elite pack.
  • Tap milestones: TC and cosmetics for tapping a category, or a whole country, many times.

Rewards wait in the Binder. A card with a green frame has something to claim: tap it (TAP TO CLAIM).

When is a country complete?

When you have caught every category that currently has live assets in that country. A category with no live assets in the last two weeks is not required. Landmarks are not required either.

Landmarks

Landmarks are famous places on the map. Tap one in your range for a random pack, once every 12 hours per landmark. The first tap also adds it to the country's Landmarks card.

Tips

  • Travelling pays: every new city is a new card.
  • An agent lets you catch city cards in places you can't visit. See Map and taps.
  • The BINDER ranking in the social panel compares your collection with other players.

Numbers on this page are approximate. We tune the game economy often, and the game always shows the current values.
