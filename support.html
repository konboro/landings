Tycoon.live by Mobimetry Sp. z o.o. Last updated: 25 September 2026

Tycoon.live is a location-based game played on live public-transport data: the vehicles and infrastructure on the map are real ones, moving in real time. If something is not working, or you just want to ask us a question, this page is the place to start.

1. Contact us

Write to info@mobimetry.com. Please tell us which device and app version you are using, and roughly where you were playing — almost every issue with a live-data game depends on those three things.

You can also reach us and other players on our Discord server.

2. The map is empty or I see no vehicles nearby

This is the most common question, and it usually has one of two causes.

Location permission. The game needs your location to show what is around you. If you declined the permission, nothing will appear near you. Turn it on in your phone settings, under the entry for Tycoon.live.

Coverage. The game runs on real public-transport feeds, so it only has something to show where those feeds exist. Today that is Poland, the United Kingdom, the United States, Germany, France, Japan, Norway, Sweden, Denmark, Finland, the Netherlands and Belgium — and within them, the cities whose operators publish live data. Outside a covered city the map is correct but empty. We add operators regularly.

3. Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by the App Store or Google Play, never by us directly, and they are charged in the currency of your store account. If a purchase did not arrive, first reopen the app while online — items are granted by our server once the store confirms the payment, which can take a moment.

If it still has not arrived, email us with the store receipt and we will look it up. Refunds are handled by Apple and Google under their own policies, so refund requests have to go through the store you bought from.

4. Deleting your account

You can delete your account and its data from inside the app: Settings → Delete account. This removes your profile, your assets and your progress, and it cannot be undone.

If you cannot reach that screen — for example because you cannot log in — email us from the address on the account and we will delete it for you.

5. Usage data

Anonymous usage analytics are off unless you turn them on. The switch is in Settings → Privacy → Data & terms, under “Share usage data”, and you can change your mind at any time. What we do and do not collect is described in the Privacy Policy.

6. Lost password

On the login screen, tap Forgot? and we will email you a six-digit code. Enter the code together with a new password. If the email does not arrive, check your spam folder and confirm you are using the address you registered with.

7. Legal

The rules of the game and the data we process are set out in the Terms of Service and the Privacy Policy.
