Market: buying and selling

The MARKET tab is where you buy assets for TC and trade with other players.

In short

  • Buy free assets in MARKET, from anywhere, for TC.
  • In range, in an agent zone or a guild HQ zone it is about 25% cheaper.
  • Sell to players on the AFTERMARKET; the fee is 5%.
  • Quick sell gives back about 10% of the price.

Two parts

The MARKET tab has two parts. STORE sells free assets straight from the live map. AFTERMARKET is where players sell to each other.

Don't mix it up with the STORE tab in the bottom bar. That one sells packs, cosmetics, TC and diamonds (see Packs).

Buying assets

  • NEARBY: assets in your range, with the in-range discount.
  • VEHICLES: pick a country, a type, then a city and operator. You get a random free vehicle of that kind.
  • INFRA: EV chargers, wind turbines, stops and stations, cable cars and airports, by country.
  • Planes are grouped by airline and aircraft type.

You can buy from anywhere in the world at the full price. You can also buy a free asset straight from its card on the map.

In-range discount

Assets in your range, in your agent zone or in your guild's HQ zone cost about 25% less. The discount doesn't apply to planes, airports or player listings.

Selling to players

  • Open an asset in ASSETS, tap LIST ON MARKET and set a price in TC.
  • A listing runs for 7 days. While it is listed, the asset is held in escrow: it earns nothing and can't be quick-sold.
  • When it sells you receive 95% of the price. The other 5% is the market fee.
  • If it doesn't sell in time, or you cancel, the asset comes back to you.
  • MINE shows your own listings.
  • Stars move with the asset to the buyer.

Quick sell

Need TC now? QUICK SELL on the asset card sells it back to the game for about 10% of its current price. It can't be undone, and the asset's stars are lost. It is mostly handy for spare pack cards.

Tips

  • Check NEARBY when you are out: the discount makes a real difference on expensive assets.
  • Planes and airports cost the same everywhere, so there is no need to travel for them.
  • Before a quick sell, think about a REROLL or a market listing instead.

Numbers on this page are approximate. We tune the game economy often, and the game always shows the current values.
