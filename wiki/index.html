Tycoon.live Wiki

Everything about playing Tycoon.live: the live map, taps, your deck, packs, the Binder, guilds and more. Written for players, in the same words the game uses.

Start in 5 steps

  1. Sign in and allow location

    Create an account with Google, Apple or email, then let the game use your location while you play. Getting started

  2. Tap what's around you

    Every pin is a real vehicle or place. Tap the pins inside your range of about 500 m to earn TC. Map and taps

  3. Open your free pack

    Go to the STORE tab and claim the free Mini pack. A new one is ready every 12 hours. Packs

  4. Fill your deck

    In ASSETS, put your best assets in the ACTIVE DECK. Only deck assets earn, and they earn 24/7. Deck

  5. Come back every day

    Play the daily challenge, finish your daily bounties and keep your login streak going. Daily play

The game in one minute

The bottom bar has five tabs: MAP (the live map), MARKET (buy assets and trade with players), ASSETS (everything you own and your deck), STORE (packs, cosmetics, TC and diamonds) and BINDER (your collection). The top bar opens your profile, MILESTONES, the social panel and settings.

You earn TC (TycoonCoins) by tapping and from your deck, and spend it on assets and upgrades. Diamonds are the premium currency for packs, boosts and cosmetics.

Numbers on this page are approximate. We tune the game economy often, and the game always shows the current values.
