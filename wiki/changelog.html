What's new

What changed in Tycoon LIVE, newest first. When a change affects a rule of the game, the topic pages of this wiki are updated too.

10 November 2026

  • Branches are open for everyone. Choose a free Branch in one of 11 countries, then tap and collect Binder cards in its zone of about 600 m from anywhere, with a landmark pack every 12 hours. See Branches.
  • More Branches in other countries cost 1,000,000, 2,000,000, 5,000,000 and 10,000,000 TC. You can have up to 5.
  • Free stops, stations, piers, EV chargers, wind turbines and cable cars bought from the game inside a Branch zone cost 5 times the usual price.
  • A guild HQ can't be placed closer than 1,090 m from the centre of a Branch. The map shows a red zone while you place it.
  • Finland: the Binder no longer lists scooter cities, because their live data has stopped. Nobody loses anything they already caught.
  • Assets owned by other players are now marked on the map: vehicles, scooters and bikes, stops and stations, cable cars, airports and planes in flight. The pin has a small person badge, and the asset bar shows the owner (OWNED BY). See Map and taps.
  • You can tap other players' cable cars and their planes in flight. The tap pays you as usual.
  • Royalties now come from every kind of asset that pays for a tap, also buses, trams, trains, metro trains, river buses, planes and wind turbines. You get the full amount when the asset is in your deck, half when it is outside the deck, and nothing while it is listed on the aftermarket. See Royalties for owners.
  • The card of your own asset shows the royalty you get for one tap by another player.
  • The range circle around you is bright green in the dark theme too.
  • The game requires version 1.0.7. If it asks you to update, get the new version from the App Store or Google Play.

9 November 2026

  • Wind turbines and EV chargers owned by other players show on the map, so a charger or turbine that someone has bought no longer looks free.
  • A royalty is now a fixed amount that depends on the asset type. Metro stations, train stations and piers pay royalties too.
  • Binder: the button to claim packs for caught categories is back, and the reward of a category is shown on its page.
  • 10 new landmarks: Pałac Kultury i Nauki in Warsaw, Amsterdam Centraal, Koninklijk Paleis on Dam Square, the Rijksmuseum, Erasmusbrug in Rotterdam, the Kinderdijk windmills, Antwerpen-Centraal, the Atomium, the Grand-Place and the Belfry of Bruges. These are the first landmarks in the Netherlands and Belgium.
  • An Elite pack holds at most one plane.
  • Guilds: in the week from 9 November every guild has a BONUS ROUND. When your guild reaches 400,000 taps, every member with at least 50 counted taps gets a Mystery Gift.
  • London: ghost trains are gone from the live map.
  • Stockholm: vehicle positions refresh every 2 minutes instead of every 3.

8 November 2026

  • Poland: the number of wind turbines grew from 2,754 to 5,336.
  • Planes that had stopped flying were replaced for their owners with flying planes of the same airline, type and price.
  • An agent leaves the map as soon as it expires.
  • DETAILED HISTORY in your stats also lists rewards from the login streak, milestones, the Binder, challenges, bounties and promo codes.

7 November 2026

  • United Kingdom: 31,060 more bus stops on the map.
  • Germany and the Netherlands: missing stops were added.
  • London buses now move live on the map.
  • Offer pop-ups are switched off. The first purchase offer stays in the STORE tab only.
  • Changes to your plane deck are saved right away.

6 November 2026

  • Share a place: press and hold the map to send a place to your guild chat, to a friend or as a link. See Sharing a place.
  • The Investor, Director, Mogul and Tycoon packs are in the store for 100, 300, 500 and 999 diamonds. See Packs.
  • An airport can come only from the Tycoon pack, and the number of planes in one pack is limited.
  • A one-time first purchase offer for players who haven't bought anything yet: an Elite pack, 200 diamonds and 200,000 TC.
  • The game asks you to confirm before you buy a pack or a boost with diamonds.
  • Wind turbines have their own category in the Binder.
  • New wind turbines in Germany, Denmark, the Netherlands and Sweden.
  • Tokyo: the Toei subway and tram are live, with 142 stations.
  • A pier now taps like other stops. In range you collect the reward and see the asset bar, and the icon or name on the bar opens the pier card.
  • Deck: you can drag a card from any row of your collection. Removing a guild HQ asks you to confirm first.
  • Binder: fixes for city cards close to a border.
  • The limited pack ended on 6 November, as planned.

5 November 2026

  • Taps on your own assets outside the deck count for daily and weekly bounties.
  • Fixed taps that sometimes got stuck.
  • Planes in the store are sorted by price, and the store loads faster.
  • After the daily challenge the map returns to its tilted view.
  • Planes that no longer fly were removed from the store. The ones players owned were replaced with flying planes.

4 November 2026

  • Version 1.0.7: a faster map, an i button that explains each screen, an update prompt inside the game and quick actions from your phone's home screen.
  • Invite links and tycoon.live links open the game.
  • SWAPS: trade 10 cards you don't need for a pack. See Packs.
  • The game asks you to confirm a purchase for TC. You can switch this off.
  • Planes: the size of the aircraft now affects its price and what it earns.

3 November 2026

  • Planes: kilometres add up step by step during the flight.
  • Map: company names above player pins and the IN RANGE view.
  • Deck: sort your cards by TC/H.
  • A limited pack in the store until 6 November.
  • Piers: a pier card with live boat traffic, and the crossed-out price when the pier is in your range.
  • A vehicle that is no longer in the live data disappears from the map when you tap it.
  • The Binder is organised by category, and notifications come in the language of the app.
  • iOS: game sounds follow the mute switch.

2 November 2026

  • Binder: an Elite pack for catching all landmarks of a country.
  • Market: a listing runs for 14 days, and MINE shows how long it has left.
  • Your stats show how many cards are in your deck.
  • Packs inside store bundles have an i button with their odds.
  • Android: fixed a crash.

Small fixes are not listed. Numbers are the ones from the day of the entry, and the game always shows the current values.

Last updated: 10 November 2026.
