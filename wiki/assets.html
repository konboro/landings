Assets

Everything you can own in Tycoon.live is a real thing: a real bus on its route, a real wind turbine, a real airport. Each asset has one owner in the whole game.

In short

  • Vehicles and infrastructure are real things from live and open data.
  • Each asset belongs to only one player in the whole game.
  • Transport vehicles also earn per kilometre; sharing vehicles earn royalties.
  • If a real vehicle disappears, your card moves to another one.

Vehicles

CategoryHow it earns
BusHourly income plus every kilometre the real bus drives
TramHourly income plus kilometres
TrainHourly income plus kilometres, often over long distances
MetroHourly income plus kilometres
River busHourly income plus kilometres on the water
PlaneHourly income plus the kilometres of its real flights, up to a daily cap
Scooter, Bike, MopedHourly income plus royalties when other players tap them (no kilometres)

Infrastructure

CategoryHow it earns
EV chargerHourly income that grows with charging power, plus royalties from taps
Wind turbineHourly income that grows with turbine power and follows the live wind in its country
Bus stop, Tram stopSteady hourly income plus royalties from taps
Train station, Metro stationSteady hourly income
PierSteady hourly income, plus a bonus for boat arrivals where live arrival data is available
Cable carSteady hourly income plus royalties from taps
AirportHourly income plus a bonus for every real flight; on-time flights pay more than delayed ones

Landmarks are not owned. You tap them for packs and for your Binder.

Where assets come from

Vehicle positions come from the public real-time feeds of transport operators and of bike and scooter-sharing services. Stops, chargers, wind turbines, cable cars and airports come from open data. Names come from the data too, so they stay in the local language. The full list of sources and licences is on the Data sources page.

How to get assets

  • Buy them for TC in the MARKET tab, or straight from the map.
  • Find them in packs. Most planes come from packs.
  • Buy them from other players on the AFTERMARKET.

One owner per asset

Each real asset can belong to only one player in the whole game. If you own a bus, nobody else can buy that bus until you sell it.

When a real vehicle disappears

Real vehicles go out of service, park for the night or drop out of the data. Your card is not lost. After a short wait (minutes for scooters and bikes, a few hours for buses, trams and trains) it moves to another free vehicle of the same operator, city and type.

If no such vehicle is free, the card shows DORMANT. It leaves the map but keeps its hourly income, and it comes back as soon as a matching vehicle is available. Planes always stay with the same aircraft.

Asset cards

Tap any of your assets in ASSETS to open its card. It shows the rarity, income per hour, kilometres, taps, total earned and stars. From the card you can add it to your deck, upgrade it with a star, quick-sell it, list it on the market or give it a pin skin.
