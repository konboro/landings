Guilds and friends

Play together: join a guild for shared HQ zones, a guild refinery and chat, add friends and climb the rankings. Guilds have their own panel behind the GUILD button on the map.

In short

  • A guild starts with 10 member slots and gets 2 more with each level.
  • Each member sets their own tax for the guild treasury, at least the minimum the leader sets.
  • The leader can name up to 2 deputies to help run the guild.
  • The treasury pays for HQs and their daily rent. Members can tap and buy inside the HQ zone.
  • Each next HQ costs more and needs more members and a higher guild level.
  • Players from outside the guild pay a zone tax on taps in your guild's HQ zone, straight to its treasury.
  • The leader can buy a real refinery that pays into the treasury.
  • Every week the whole guild collects taps in the weekly challenge to earn packs. The goals grow with the number of members.
  • Friends, direct messages and the player ranking are in the social panel.

The GUILD button on the map opens the guild panel. A player in a guild sees their guild and the RANKINGS there. A player without a guild has the GUILD LIST, CREATE GUILD and INVITES tabs. The CHAT button below it opens the chat. Friends, the player ranking and alerts are in the social panel in the top bar.

Joining a guild

  • The GUILD LIST shows all guilds. You can sort it by level or by members. Tap JOIN. In a guild marked INSTANT JOIN you become a member right away. In the other guilds JOIN sends a request, and the guild's leader or a deputy approves or rejects it. The list also shows each guild's minimum tax. Tap REQUEST SENT again to cancel.
  • The leader chooses how players join in the JOINING tile on the guild's page: INSTANT, which is the default, or BY APPROVAL.
  • A leader can also invite you by your company name. Invites appear under INVITES, and accepting one lets you in straight away. Invites work in both modes.
  • A guild has 10 member slots at level 1. Each new level adds 2 more, so a level 10 guild has room for 28 and a level 30 guild for 68. You can only be in one guild at a time.

Creating a guild

Creating a guild costs 50,000 TC. The name has 3 to 24 characters and must be unique. The tag has 2 to 5 letters or digits, and the description up to 120 characters. Every new guild starts with the default emblem and colour.

Roles

  • Leader: edits the guild's name, description, emblem and colour, invites players, sets the minimum tax, names deputies and can disband the guild. Only the leader buys in the guild shop and buys or sells a refinery. The leader can also do everything a deputy can.
  • Deputy: a guild can have up to 2. The leader names one with the shield button on a member's card. A deputy approves or rejects join requests, removes regular members and gives them ranks, places, moves and removes HQs and sees the activity view.
  • Member: sets their own tax, uses the guild chat and the HQ zone, and can leave at any time.
  • Ranks such as "Officer" are titles the leader or a deputy gives. They don't add any permissions.

The MEMBERS list shows a card for every member: logo with an online dot, tag and company name, role and rank, when they were last in the game, the tax they paid since joining, their taps and their tax rate. The message button on a card opens a private chat with that player.

Activity view

The leader and deputies can switch the MEMBERS list from LIST to ACTIVITY. It shows this week for every member: taps in the HQ zone that count for the weekly challenge, all paid taps, tax paid to the treasury and weekly bounties done. The week runs from Monday to Sunday, Polish time.

Treasury and tax

Each member chooses their own tax, from 0% to 50%. Set it with minus and plus in the YOUR TAX tile on your guild's page, 1% per tap. That share of your hourly income and taps goes to the guild's treasury. The treasury pays for HQs and their rent, for the guild shop and for refineries.

The TREASURY tile on your guild's page opens the TREASURY HISTORY. On top are the tiles for this week: income, spending, balance, HQ tax, member tax with the top payer and the best day, plus a chart of daily income. The arrows next to the heading switch to earlier weeks. Below are the member ranking, by tax or by taps, and the list of entries, which you can filter with ALL, IN, OUT and EVENTS.

The leader can set a GUILD MINIMUM in the same place, in steps of 5%, currently up to 50%. Members below it pay the minimum from that moment and get a notification. You can always pay more than the minimum, but not less. If the leader lowers the minimum, your own setting comes back.

All the tax a guild has ever paid raises its level, from LVL 1 to LVL 30. A higher level means more member slots and a bigger HQ zone. Levels above 10 take much longer:

Guild HQ

The leader or a deputy places HQs on the map with TC from the treasury. Inside the HQ zone, every member can tap assets and buy at the in-range discount, even far from home. Those taps count for bounties and the Binder.

A guild can have up to 6 HQs. Each next one costs more, and it unlocks only when the guild has both enough members and a high enough level:

  • Rent. Every HQ costs 0.5% of its price per day. The treasury pays once a day. If it can't cover all the rent, the most expensive HQs switch off first: members can't use their zones and they collect no zone tax. They switch back on by themselves within the hour once the treasury can cover the rent, or right away when the leader or a deputy taps Pay rent on the HQ's card.
  • Moving. An HQ can be moved for 10% of the price of the guild's latest HQ, at least 100,000 TC. With one HQ that is 100,000 TC, with six it is 15 million TC.
  • Removing. The leader or a deputy can remove an HQ. There is no refund.
  • Zone. The zone is about 200 m at level 1 and grows by about 10 m per level, up to about 490 m at level 30.
  • Distance from other guilds. An HQ can't be placed closer than 980 m to another guild's HQ, so zones of different guilds never overlap. Your own HQs can stand close together. The map shows red circles around other guilds' HQs while you place yours.
  • Distance from Branches. An HQ can't be placed closer than 1,090 m to the centre of a Branch. The map shows a red zone around every Branch while you place it.
  • Names. The leader or a deputy can give each HQ a name of up to 6 characters. It shows on the HQ's card and on your HQ button on the map.
  • On the map. HQ pins of all guilds are visible. Hold a pin to see the guild's card.

The HQ section of the guild panel shows the current price list, rent and requirements under HQ rules.

Zone tax

When a player from outside the guild taps an asset inside your guild's HQ zone, 10% of the TC from that tap goes to your guild's treasury. It works the same for taps made on the spot and through an agent. Members pay no zone tax in their own guild's zones.

When you are inside another guild's zone, the top of the map shows that guild's emblem, its tag and the tax.

Refineries

Refineries are the only asset a guild can own. They are real refineries in the USA, Japan and Europe, about 170 in total, and each one has a single owner in the whole game.

  • Buying. Only the leader buys, with TC from the treasury, in the SHOP section of the guild panel.
  • Price. It depends on the real refining capacity: 24 million TC plus 1.2 million TC for every 1,000 barrels a day, never below 30 million and never above 900 million TC. Today the cheapest costs 37 million TC and the most expensive 811.5 million TC.
  • Income. A refinery pays into the treasury every 15 minutes. At an average margin a refinery pays for itself in about 50 days, the largest ones in 60 days at the latest. Once a week the income changes by a multiplier from ×0.8 to ×1.2, following real refining margins (Brent crude, gasoline and diesel). On top of that comes the market of the refinery's country, so together the multiplier ranges from ×0.75 to ×1.3.
  • Slots. A guild can own 1 refinery and 1 more for every 10 guild levels: 2 from level 10, 3 from level 20 and 4 at level 30.
  • Selling. The leader can sell a refinery back to the game for half the purchase price, 7 days after buying at the earliest. Guilds can't trade refineries with each other.
  • If the guild is disbanded, its refinery goes back to the pool and nothing is paid out.

Guild shop

The SHOP section also sells looks for the guild. The leader pays once from the treasury and the guild keeps the item.

  • Emblems: 9 regular ones for 6 million TC each and 5 epic ones for 20 million TC each.
  • Legendary sets, an emblem with its own colour: 2 sets for 60 million TC each.
  • Colours: 6 for 1 million TC each and 4 premium ones for 4 million TC each.
  • A guild's emblem and colour come only from the shop. A new guild starts with the default look. An existing guild keeps its current look until the leader changes it, and an old look that is not in the shop cannot be picked again afterwards.

Weekly challenge

Every week the whole guild adds taps to one shared counter. You find it under WEEKLY TASK on your guild's page in the guild panel. The tasks panel on the map has a GUILD tab with the same task: the guild's counter, the tiers with their packs, your place in the guild and the most active members. A player without a guild sees an invitation to join one there.

  • Members' taps that earn TC count when the tapped asset is within 2 km of any of the guild's HQs. You can tap on the spot or remotely, in an HQ or agent zone.
  • The goals depend on the size of the guild. The tiers are BRONZE at 1,000 taps per member (Standard pack), SILVER at 3,500 per member (Premium pack) and GOLD at 10,000 per member (Elite pack). A guild of 10 needs 10,000, 35,000 and 100,000 taps.
  • The game counts a guild with fewer than 3 members as a guild of 3. The higher of two numbers sets the goal: the members on Monday or the members now, so removing members during the week doesn't lower it. A tier already reached stays reached, even if new members raise the goal later. The panel shows for how many members the goal is set.
  • Each tier gives a pack to every member with at least 50 of their own counted taps that week. If you reach 50 later, the packs for tiers already reached arrive at that moment.
  • You only get packs for tiers reached after you joined the guild.
  • Packs wait under YOUR FREE PACKS in the STORE tab, and you get a notification at every tier.
  • The counter resets on Monday at midnight Polish time.

Now and then the game team adds an extra tier for one week, for one guild or for all of them, such as a BONUS ROUND with a Mystery Gift for every member who qualifies. The same rules apply as for the other tiers.

Chat

The CHAT button on the map opens the chat with three tabs:

  • GUILD: visible only to the guild's members. It shows the newest 300 messages; scroll up to load older ones. Announcements from the game team appear here too, signed Tycoon.Live.
  • PRIVATE: your direct messages with friends and other players.
  • HELP: a channel for all players to ask about the game. Tap a player's name to reply to them. A card at the top opens this wiki.

Messages in HELP are kept for 7 days. Links typed by players are never clickable. Only links in announcements from Tycoon.Live open.

Leaving and disbanding

  • Anyone can leave. If the leader leaves, a deputy becomes the new leader, or without a deputy the member who joined earliest. If the last member leaves, the guild is closed.
  • A removed member can join again.
  • Disbanding removes all members, HQs and the chat for good. A refinery goes back to the pool with no payout.

Friends and messages

Tap the people icon in the top-right corner. In FRIENDS, open INVITES, search for a player by ticker or name and tap ＋ ADD. They accept or reject your request. Requests from other players appear in the same tab: tap the green tick to accept one. Each friend shows when they were last in the game: ONLINE, or for example 5 MIN AGO or 2 DAYS AGO. Guild members show the same. Tap the envelope to send a direct message.

Rankings

Guilds have their own RANKINGS in the guild panel: INCOME (what came into the treasury since Monday) and TAPS (taps by all members since Monday). Both start from zero every Monday, and your own guild is highlighted with its place.

Player ranking: RANKING in the social panel shows the top 100 players by INCOME (total TC earned), TAPS or BINDER. The daily challenge has its own daily ranking (see Daily play).

Player profiles

Tap a player's logo on the map, in your friends list, your guild or the ranking to see their profile: net worth, income, kilometres, their active deck, portfolio and Binder. The active deck shows the vehicles and the infrastructure the player has in play. The portfolio is folded, tap it to open. A profile never shows a player's TC balance.

Invite friends

Settings, then INVITE FRIENDS, shows your referral code. A friend who enters it on an account up to 7 days old gets a free pack. You earn packs as your friends secure their account and play, for up to 25 friends.

Numbers on this page are approximate. We tune the game economy often, and the game always shows the current values.
