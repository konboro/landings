FAQ and glossary

Quick answers to the questions players ask most, and a glossary of the words the game uses.

FAQ

Is Tycoon.live free?

Yes. The game is free to download and play. Optional purchases let you buy diamonds and TC. See Diamonds and ads.

Do I have to go outside to play?

Tapping needs you to be within about 500 m of an asset, so walking around pays off. Your deck earns wherever you are, you can buy assets from anywhere, and agents and guild HQs let you tap from a distance.

Do my assets earn when the app is closed?

Yes, if they are in your active deck. The deck earns 24/7 with no time limit. See Active deck.

Why does tapping my own asset pay nothing?

Your own assets pay for taps only when they are in your active deck. Add the asset to the deck, or tap free and other players' assets, which always pay.

My vehicle disappeared from the map. Did I lose it?

No. When a real vehicle leaves the data, your card moves to another free vehicle of the same operator, city and type. If none is free it shows DORMANT and keeps earning hourly income until one appears. See Assets.

Why can't I buy a vehicle I see on the map?

Every asset has only one owner. If another player owns it, you can only buy it if they list it on the aftermarket.

Where can I see the pack odds?

In the game: tap the i button on any pack in the STORE tab to open the Odds screen before you buy.

How can I play with friends in another city?

Join the same guild and place an HQ in a place you all want to play, or drop an agent there. See Guilds and friends and Map and taps.

How do I hide from other players?

Settings, then VISIBILITY, then switch off SHOW ME ON MAP. You won't see other players either, and player taps are off. See Getting started.

What happens to my stars if I sell an asset?

They are lost. Quick sell removes them, and a buyer on the market starts with no stars.

Can I win real money or own the real vehicle?

No. TC and diamonds are in-game currencies only, and owning an asset in the game gives you no rights to the real vehicle or building.

I need help with my account or a purchase.

Visit the Support page or write to info@mobimetry.com. To delete your account, see the account deletion page.

Glossary

Active deck
Up to 10 vehicles and 10 infrastructure assets that earn hourly income. Only deck assets earn. Active deck
Aftermarket
The part of the MARKET tab where players sell assets to each other. Market: buying and selling
Agent
A temporary zone of about 500 m you can drop anywhere to tap and buy there. Map and taps
Asset
Anything you can own: a vehicle or a piece of infrastructure, always a real one.
Binder
Your collection album, filled by tapping real assets on the map. Binder
Boost
A paid power-up for 30 minutes to 8 hours: double income or a wider reach.
Cooldown
The wait before you can tap the same thing again: 5 minutes for an asset, 12 hours for a landmark, 1 hour for a player.
Diamonds
The premium currency, for packs, boosts, agents and cosmetics. Diamonds and ads
Dormant
A vehicle card whose real vehicle is gone and has no free replacement yet. It keeps its hourly income.
Escrow
An asset listed on the market is held aside and earns nothing until it sells or you cancel.
Ghost mode
Hiding yourself from other players with SHOW ME ON MAP switched off.
HQ
A guild headquarters. Members can tap and buy inside its zone. Guilds and friends
Landmark
A famous place on the map. Tap it for a pack every 12 hours.
Milestone
A long-term goal on one of five tracks, with TC, cosmetics, diamonds and packs as rewards.
Quick sell
Selling an asset back to the game for about 10% of its price.
Range
How far you can tap: about 500 m around you, or about 1 km with a boost.
Rarity
Common, Rare, Epic or Legendary. Rarer assets cost and earn more. Rarity and stars
REROLL
Exchanging 5 vehicles of the same type for a pack.
Royalty
The share of a tap reward you get when another player taps certain assets you own.
Star
An upgrade that raises everything one asset earns. Up to 3 per asset.
Tap
Touching a pin in your range to collect TC.
TC (TycoonCoins)
The main in-game currency, earned by your deck and by tapping.
Treasury
A guild's shared money, filled by member taxes and spent on HQs.
