Power plants

Power plants are a new asset category. You buy blocks of real power plants, and a block's income goes up and down with the power it really produces.

In short

  • Blocks of real power plants in 10 countries. 444 blocks are for sale at launch.
  • All power plants are legendary.
  • The price depends on a block's average output, from about 9.5 million to about 246 million TC.
  • A block pays back in about 36 days, and its income follows live output.
  • Merge blocks of one plant into one stronger card.

Where the power plants are

You can buy power plant blocks in 10 countries:

  • Belgium
  • Denmark
  • Finland
  • France
  • Germany
  • Netherlands
  • Norway
  • Poland
  • Sweden
  • United Kingdom

The blocks and their output come from grid operators: the ENTSO-E platform, PSE in Poland and Elexon in the United Kingdom. 444 blocks are for sale at launch. Some UK gas blocks come later.

A big power plant usually has several blocks, the separate units that produce power. You buy them one by one. Each block has one owner in the whole game.

Buying a block

In the MARKET tab, in STORE, pick INFRA, a country, then POWER PLANTS. Blocks are grouped by power plant, and each plant shows how many blocks are still free. A block you buy goes to ASSETS as a power plant card.

  • Power plants are not in packs.
  • Buy offers between players are not available yet.

Block price

The price depends on how much the block produces on average, not on the power on its nameplate. A block costs about 8 million TC plus 0.16 million TC for each MW of average output, rounded to 0.5 million TC. So a block that averages 100 MW costs about 24 million TC.

The cheapest blocks cost about 9.5 million TC and the biggest about 246 million TC.

What a block earns

Every block has an income plan. The plan is set so that the block pays back its price in about 36 days. The biggest blocks take a bit longer, up to about 43 days.

The payout follows the block's real output:

  • Producing its average: you get the plan.
  • Producing more: you get more, up to 1.8 times the plan.
  • Producing less: you get less.
  • Standing idle: you still get 20% of the plan.

Exactly: payout = plan × (0.2 + 0.8 × output now ÷ average output), at most 1.8 × plan.

The block card shows the plan, and the TC/h in your deck header shows what you get right now.

Merging blocks

Got several blocks of the same power plant? Merge them into one stronger card. Open the power plant card and tap MERGE.

  • Each block you add gives a +8% bonus.
  • A complete set, all blocks of the plant on one card, adds another +25%.
  • The whole bonus is capped at 60% and at 12 million TC per day.
  • The bonus follows real output too, just like the block's income.
  • The merge fee is 8% of the price of the blocks you add. You never pay twice for a block that is already on the card.

The card shows the bonus as CARD BONUS. Merging can't be undone: an added block stays on the card for good.

Power plants on the map

  • Zoom in and every block has its own pin.
  • Zoom out and you see one pin per power plant, with the number of blocks.
  • A gold pin is your block. A brown pin is a free block or another player's. Another player's block has a small person badge on its pin.
  • Power plant pins can wear skins like any other asset. See Cosmetics.

Deck, market and selling

  • Add a power plant to your deck like any other asset. It earns in the infrastructure deck. See Deck.
  • You can sell it to players with LIST ON MARKET. Power plants have their own POWER PLANTS category on the aftermarket.
  • QUICK SELL gives back 5% of the price you paid, plus the earnings accrued so far. It can't be undone.

Numbers on this page are the current ones. The game always shows the values in force.
