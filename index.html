How it plays

The map is the real world

No made-up cities. Tycoon.live puts live public transport and real infrastructure on your map, and turns all of it into things you can own.

01 · Tap

Tap what's really around you

Every pin is a real bus, tram, train, scooter or bike at its real position, refreshed from live transit feeds. Get within 500 metres and tap it to earn TC.

  • Live positions from public transport and bike and scooter-sharing feeds
  • Tap any vehicle or station within 500 m, again every 5 minutes
  • Every new category you catch goes into your Binder
02 · Own

Own the real thing

Buy the actual tram rolling down your street or a wind turbine on the edge of town. Put it in your deck and it earns for you every hour, day and night.

  • Your deck earns 24/7, even while the app is closed
  • Buses, trams and trains also earn for every real kilometre they drive
  • Wind turbines follow the live wind, airports follow real flights
03 · Grow

Build a global portfolio

Start with one scooter and end up with metro trains, airliners and airports across twelve countries. Upgrade your best assets, trade with other players and climb the rankings.

  • Star upgrades raise what an asset earns
  • A player market for buying and selling cards
  • Leaderboards, milestones and daily rewards

FAQ

Questions

Is Tycoon.live free?

Yes. Tycoon.live is free to download and play. Optional in-app purchases let you buy diamonds, the premium in-game currency.

Do I have to go outside to play?

Tapping needs you to be within about 500 m of a vehicle or station, so walking around pays off. Everything you own keeps earning wherever you are, and you can buy assets from the in-game store from anywhere.

Is the data really live?

Yes. Vehicle positions come from public real-time transport and mobility feeds and refresh continuously. Wind turbines earn according to live national wind generation, and airports according to real flight schedules.

Can I win real money or own the real vehicle?

No. TC and diamonds are in-game currencies only. They can't be exchanged for money or anything outside the game, and owning an asset in the game gives you no rights to the real vehicle or building.

Which countries are on the map?

The live map covers the United States, Germany, Norway, France, the United Kingdom, Poland, Belgium, Japan, the Netherlands, Denmark, Sweden and Finland. Whether you can download the game depends on your App Store or Google Play region.

What does the game do with my location?

It uses your location while you play, to show what's around you and to check that you're close enough to tap. Other players can see you on the map unless you switch on ghost mode. Details are in our Privacy Policy.

I need help with my account or a purchase

Visit our Support page or write to info@mobimetry.com. You can also find us on Discord.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Settings, then Account actions, and tap Delete account. The full steps, including what to do if you can't log in, are on our account deletion page.
