Tycoon.live is played on live, real-world data. The vehicles, stops, wind turbines, chargers and airports in the game come from public-transport operators, open-data portals and community projects. This page lists them, with the licence each one is published under. We are grateful to all of them.

Tycoon.live is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by any of the organisations listed here. The data is retrieved continuously and processed for the game: positions can be delayed, incomplete or wrong, and some vehicles (for example the London Underground, the Paris metro and long-distance trains in France and Germany) are placed on the map from live arrival predictions and timetables rather than from GPS. Never use Tycoon.live to plan a journey; check the operator's own information instead.

9. Wind and energy

Wind turbines in the game earn according to how much wind power their country is producing right now.

  • Poland and Germany: ENTSO-E Transparency Platform.
  • Great Britain: Elexon BMRS / Insights Solution. Contains BMRS data © Elexon Limited copyright and database right 2026.
  • Japan: Weather data by Open-Meteo.com, CC BY 4.0.
