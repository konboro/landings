Getting started

Tycoon LIVE is a free location-based tycoon game. The map shows real public transport and real infrastructure around you, and you can own it.

In short

  • Sign in with Google, Apple (iPhone) or email.
  • Location is used only while the game is open.
  • Hide from other players with ghost mode.
  • Analytics are off until you switch them on.

Create an account

When you open the game for the first time, you sign in. You can continue with Google, with Apple on an iPhone, or with an email address and a password of at least 6 characters. Your progress is saved on our servers, so you can log in on a new phone and carry on where you left off.

Forgot your password? On the login screen tap Forgot password? and we email you a 6-digit code to set a new one.

Older guest accounts

New guest accounts are no longer created. If you still play on an old guest account, the game reminds you to secure it: open Settings and tap SECURE ACCOUNT. You keep everything you own. A guest account that logs out, or a phone where the game is reinstalled, loses all its progress.

Location

The game uses your location only while it is open. It shows what is around you and checks that you are close enough to tap something. Without location the map is view-only: you can look around, but you can't collect.

You don't have to be outside all the time. Your deck keeps earning wherever you are, and you can buy assets from anywhere. See Earning TC.

Your company

In Tycoon LIVE you run a company. Tap your company emblem in the top bar to open your PROFILE, then tap EDIT to choose the company name, logo and motto. Other players see your company name on the map, in guilds and in the rankings.

Ghost mode

Other players see your approximate position on the map, rounded to about 100 m. To hide, open Settings and, under VISIBILITY, switch off SHOW ME ON MAP.

  • While hidden, you also can't see other players on the map.
  • Player taps are off both ways.
  • Tapping vehicles and places works as usual.

Privacy and settings

  • SHARE USAGE DATA is off until you switch it on. It never sends your email, company name, exact location or advertising ID.
  • Language: SYSTEM (follows your phone), English or Polski. Vehicle, stop and city names stay in their original language.
  • Theme: dark, light, or auto, which follows sunrise and sunset where you are.
  • To delete your account, open Settings and tap DELETE ACCOUNT. This removes your assets, currencies and progress for good. Full steps are on the account deletion page.

Everything about your data is in our Privacy Policy.

What next?
