Delete your account

Tycoon.live by Mobimetry Sp. z o.o. Last updated: 25 September 2026

You can delete your Tycoon.live account and the data tied to it at any time. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

1. Delete it in the app

This is the fastest way and it takes effect immediately.

  1. Open Tycoon.live and go to Settings.
  2. Scroll to Account actions and tap Delete account.
  3. Read the warning, type DELETE to confirm, then tap Delete my account forever.

You are signed out on every device and the account is removed straight away. If you lead a guild that still has other members, leadership passes to its longest-standing member; a guild with no other members is removed together with your account.

2. Ask us by e-mail

If you cannot sign in and therefore cannot reach that screen, write to us from the e-mail address the account uses:

  • To: info@mobimetry.com
  • Subject: Account deletion — Tycoon.live
  • Include: your in-game company name and how you signed in (e-mail, Google or Apple)

We may ask for more information to confirm the account is yours. Requests are handled without undue delay and within the time limits set by applicable data-protection law.

3. What gets deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes:

  • your profile, login credentials and company name;
  • every vehicle, piece of infrastructure and other virtual asset;
  • in-game currency balances (TC and diamonds), including any that were purchased;
  • binder collection, achievements, progress, rankings and statistics;
  • guild membership, market listings, friends, messages and other social data;
  • device identifiers, push tokens and app session data tied to the account.

Virtual currency and items have no monetary value and are not refunded on deletion — see the Terms of Service, sections 10 and 18.4.

4. What we may keep, and for how long

After deletion we keep only what the law requires or what we need to protect our rights — for example purchase records we must hold for accounting and tax purposes, or information needed to prevent fraud and to establish, pursue or defend legal claims. That data is kept for as long as the purpose requires and then deleted. See the Privacy Policy, sections 10 and 14.
