2. What data do we collect and how do we use it?

The scope of the data we process depends on how you use Tycoon.live and which features you use.

When creating an Account, we may receive information such as your email address, user ID, in-game name, age or date of birth information, and information required to authenticate your Account. If you log in using an external provider, such as Google, we may receive information shared with us by that provider in accordance with your privacy settings and its privacy policy.

While using the Game, we may collect information concerning your activity, including gameplay information, assets owned, transactions carried out in the Game, progress, results, rankings, achievements, use of Game features, social interactions, and other activities performed through your Account.

We may also receive technical information concerning your device and connection to the Service, such as device type, operating system, application version, device identifiers, IP address, network information, language, regional settings, crash data, and information concerning how the application is used.

We primarily use this information to provide, maintain, and develop Tycoon.live, enable login and use of your Account, save game progress, ensure the proper operation of features, respond to user requests, and ensure the security of the Service.

We may also analyze how Tycoon.live is used in order to improve Game features, fix errors, develop new features, and better understand how our Service is used.

3. Location and Real-World Data

Tycoon.live uses a real-world map and data concerning real-world objects and infrastructure. Depending on the Game feature, we may use device location data or other location-related information where this is necessary for the operation of a particular feature.

If a feature requires access to your device's location, we may ask you for the appropriate permission. You can change your settings regarding the application's access to your location at any time in your device settings; however, this may cause certain Game features to stop working or to operate with limited functionality.

Location data may be used, among other things, to determine the user's position on the map, associate activity with a specific geographic area, enable the use of location-based features, and ensure the proper operation of the Game mechanics.

Tycoon.live may also use data concerning real-world objects, such as stations, stops, airports, ports, sports facilities, vehicles, energy infrastructure, or other elements of the real world. Such data may come from external providers and may not constitute personal data.

4. Security, Fraud and Proper Operation of the Game

The security of Tycoon.live and fair gameplay are important to us. Accordingly, we may process information concerning how the Game is used in order to detect fraud, abuse, unauthorized access, data manipulation, the use of bots, modified software, or other activities that violate the Terms of Service.

We may analyze technical information, Account information, Game activity information, and device data where necessary to detect and prevent fraud, protect our systems, and protect other users.

Where justified, we may use the services of specialized security and anti-fraud technology providers.

Such processing may be necessary for the performance of the agreement with the user, to ensure the security of the Service, or to pursue our legitimate interest in protecting Tycoon.live against fraud and abuse.

5. Purchase and Payment Data

Tycoon.live may allow users to make in-app purchases, including purchases of virtual currency, virtual items, packages, or other digital products.

If you make a purchase, we may receive information necessary to confirm and process the transaction, such as the transaction ID, purchased product, price, currency, purchase date, and information concerning the payment status.

Payment instrument data, such as the full card number, may be processed directly by the app store or an external payment provider. In such cases, Tycoon.live may not receive the full card details.

We use purchase information to process transactions, provide access to purchased content, handle refunds and complaints, maintain accounting records, and prevent unauthorized transactions and fraud.

Purchases may also be subject to the privacy policies and terms of the relevant app store or payment provider.

6. Social Features and Communication

Tycoon.live may provide features that allow users to interact with other people, such as friends, guilds, rankings, profiles, trading, messages, or other social features.

If you use such features, certain information related to your profile or activity may be visible to other users in accordance with the settings of the relevant feature.

Such information may include, among other things, your username, profile elements, results, level, achievements, ranking, owned assets, or other information related to gameplay.

We recommend that when creating a username and profile, you do not use information that makes it easy to determine your real-world identity, such as your full name, residential address, or telephone number.

If you contact our support team, we may process the content of your message, your Account details, and information necessary to resolve your request.
