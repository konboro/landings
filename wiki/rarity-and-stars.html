Rarity and stars

Every asset has a rarity: COMMON, RARE, EPIC or LEGENDARY. Stars are upgrades you unlock on a single asset to make it earn more.

In short

  • Four rarities: Common, Rare, Epic, Legendary.
  • Rarer assets cost more and earn more per hour.
  • Up to 3 stars per asset; each star adds a bonus to all its earnings.
  • A star needs the asset to have earned enough, plus a TC payment.

Rarity

Rarity depends on the asset type, and for EV chargers on their charging power. Rarer assets cost more and earn more per hour. On the map, rarer pins are slightly bigger.

RarityExamples today
COMMONScooters, bikes, mopeds, buses, bus stops, EV chargers under 100 kW
RARETrams, tram stops, EV chargers from 100 kW
EPICTrains, metro trains, river buses, wind turbines, train and metro stations, piers, EV chargers from 200 kW
LEGENDARYPlanes, cable cars, airports

We sometimes move a type to another rarity when we rebalance the game. The card always shows the current rarity.

Stars

Each asset can get up to 3 stars. A star adds a bonus to everything the asset earns: hourly income, kilometres, bonuses and the royalties you receive. The bonus is bigger for rarer assets.

RarityBonus per starWith 3 stars
Common+8%+24%
Rare+12%+36%
Epic+16%+48%
Legendary+20%+60%

How to get a star

A star has two conditions, both measured in that asset's own daily income:

  • Earned: the asset must have earned enough TC since you got it. About one day of its income for the first star, about a month for the second and about three months for the third.
  • Price: you pay TC, about 8, 25 and 70 days of the asset's income for stars 1, 2 and 3.

Open the asset card to see the progress (EARNED x / y) and the price on the CLAIM ★ button.

Stars belong to you, not the card

Stars are lost when you quick-sell an asset, and a player who buys your asset on the market starts with no stars.

Numbers on this page are approximate. We tune the game economy often, and the game always shows the current values.
