Earning TC

TC (TycoonCoins) is the main currency of the game. Your deck earns it around the clock, and you earn more on the move by tapping.

In short

  • Your deck earns hourly income 24/7, even with the app closed.
  • Transport vehicles also earn for every real kilometre.
  • Taps pay on the spot; owners of some assets get royalties.
  • Most assets pay back their price in about 4 to 12 days.

Income from your deck

  • Every asset in your ACTIVE DECK earns an hourly income, all day and all night, even when the app is closed. There is no limit on how long you can be away.
  • Assets outside the deck don't earn hourly income. See Active deck.
  • When you come back, the Welcome back! screen shows what your fleet earned. You can watch an optional ad for +20% on that payout.

Kilometres

Buses, trams, trains, metro trains, river buses and planes also earn for every kilometre the real vehicle travels while it is in your deck. Kilometres travelled while the asset was outside the deck are not paid later. Scooters, bikes and mopeds don't earn kilometres. Planes have a daily kilometre cap.

Taps and royalties

A tap pays a fixed amount by asset type, currently about 80 to 160 TC, and the same asset can be tapped again after 5 minutes. If another player taps your scooter, bike, moped, EV charger, bus or tram stop or cable car, you get a royalty of about a quarter of their reward. Details in Map and taps.

Live data changes your income

  • Wind turbines follow the live wind in their country, updated about every 15 minutes. A windy day pays more. Income grows with turbine power up to a cap of 3,000 kW, so the very largest turbines pay back more slowly.
  • Airports earn a bonus for each real arrival and departure. On-time flights pay more than delayed ones.
  • Piers earn a bonus for boat arrivals where live arrival data is available.
  • EV chargers earn more the more powerful they are, up to a cap.

How long until an asset pays for itself

Rarer assets cost more and take longer to earn back their price, but they earn more per hour. Counting only hourly income at the full list price, it takes roughly:

RarityPays back its price in about
Common4 to 5 days
Rare5 to 7 days
Epic5 to 12 days (wind turbines about 8 to 16, depending on the wind)
Legendary3 weeks for cable cars, 5 weeks for airports, 1 to 3 months for planes

Kilometres, royalties, bonuses and stars make it faster, and so does buying in range with the discount. Assets from packs cost you nothing, so they are profit from day one.

Stars, boosts and guild tax

  • Stars raise everything an asset earns. See Rarity and stars.
  • The ×2 INCOME boost doubles what you collect while it is active, for 30 minutes, 2 hours or 8 hours. It costs diamonds.
  • In a guild, the tax rate you choose goes from your hourly income and taps to the guild treasury. See Guilds and friends.

What TC is for

Buying assets, star upgrades, creating a guild and buying from other players. TC is an in-game currency only and can't be exchanged for money.

Numbers on this page are approximate. We tune the game economy often, and the game always shows the current values.
